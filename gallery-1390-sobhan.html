Group Exhibition, Sobhan Gallery (2011)

Gender
This collection views gender not as a fixed essence, but as an identity shaped through interaction with the body, culture, and social structures.

Flowers, with their living and mutable nature, serve as a metaphor for the body, while resin, through its transparency and rigidity, freezes these forms at a specific moment—simultaneously preserving and constraining identity.
