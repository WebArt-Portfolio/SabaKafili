My artistic practice is grounded in material experimentation and the exploration of the expressive potentials of various mediums. Depending on the concept of each project, I work with painting, collage, or volumetric elements, and sometimes combine these mediums together. For me, choosing the medium is part of idea formation, and every artwork demands its own visual language. Nature serves as one of the primary sources of inspiration in my work; not merely as a subject, but as a framework to study texture, growth, transformation, erosion, and the relationships between elements. I am interested in discovering novel sensory and visual qualities through trial and error with various materials, and testing the different possibilities of each medium in the process of creation. To me, the process of creation is as important as the outcome. Experience, discovery, and interaction with materials form the core of my artistic approach, and each project is an opportunity for a deeper understanding of the relationship between nature, matter, and visual expression.
